## Why not log clipping or automatic whole-tree felling now

Removing only room-intersecting trunksegments would leave floating upperlogs/canopy and weakens the provenance problem. Do not do it. If native sampling confirms unplanned interiorlogs, the subsequent smallest honest option is a separately reviewed full SINGLE rooted-tree proof within the same maintenance feature, not a generic forest clearer: all actual naturalconsistent logs/branches connected to the seed, all support/rootcolumns and adjacentcontacts, untouched plannedtimber excluded, fullsnapshot/CAS, strict finite treecap/halo and no independent-root/foreign/protected/orecontact. Any component continuation beyondcap/halo, intertwined root or protectedbranch preserves the WHOLE component (0logwrites); no enlarged4000blockforest pass merely to improve the room. Originalground treeflag plus exact currentlyowned foundation can support only a limited currentrootbase provenance check; neither proves all arbitrary logs natural. This extension is BLOCKED on actuallogcoordinates/topology and all-source ownership, not included in firstleafslice. Root may decide after inspection that logs are plannedposts and no fellingfeature is needed.
