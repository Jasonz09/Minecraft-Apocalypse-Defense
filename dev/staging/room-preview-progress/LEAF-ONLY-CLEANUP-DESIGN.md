# Next chapel leaf-only cleanup — DESIGN, no mutator

The current loaded census has39 discrepancies, with only page1's32 rows manually
transcribed:22 logs and10 oak leaves. Seven other discrepancies have not yet been
classified. Logs are now located, but their provenance, complete connectivity and
roots remain unknown. Keep every log intact. The existing wall-leaf journal and
saved room AIR are not room-cleanup authority.

The next bounded evidence step is source/native classification of the candidate
leaf cells and their immediate27-cell neighborhoods, keeping exact primitive ID
and full-state snapshots. Read only verified completed source records sequentially;
discard each heavy record after compact relevant final/historical claims are
collected. Check all five district sources, exact master/checksum/queues/dimensions,
protection provenance and every later write at the requested cells. No old census
row is a live capability; every page invocation is a separate time-distributed
observation, and future action needs a new guarded proof.

Before any separately reviewed mutating policy, require:

- Targets derived from the selected chapel's explicit bounded interior/lectern
  access envelope, never a coordinate allowlist or generic missing-AIR repair.
  No whole-map canopy removal. Preserve natural greenery outside that envelope.
- Leaves only, with exact fresh readable type/full states and persistence=false.
  That state alone is not proof natural origin. Any historical planned tree/leaf
  at a target in any district, final non-air structural claim, unknown protection,
  neighboring interior/prototype or cross-record conflict refuses the target.
- Full fresh neighborhood proof preserves actual logs, all matched planned room
  timber/fixtures/supports, player/unknown blocks, attached plants and gravity or
  block-entity contacts. In particular no leaf may be removed if it supports an
  attached vine or if the source/neighborhood is unreadable or ambiguous. Own
  saved structural contacts are verified in place; their mere ID is not a bypass.
- No trunk clipping, log erasure or implied tree-component ownership. If preserving
  logs leaves the lectern unusable, keep that separate unresolved obstruction and
  investigate its exact all-source topology rather than broadening leaf cleanup.
- A new explicit leaf-pruning policy and independent typed request/journal are
  necessary before writes. Retain original leaf snapshots and source identity,
  re-prove pending targets/neighborhoods after restart, CAS the receipt and exact
  final live leaf before a single removal, then verify. Changed AIR is not a
  substitute for the original leaf. Existing v1 wall receipts remain untouched;
  no room snapshot or completed wall batch can be reinterpreted as permission.

First acceptance should be a preview-only list of a small independently proved
leaf subset, then a backed-up disposable-copy action only after root policy review.
Require unchanged logs, lantern states and supports, crops/furnishings, player
blocks and source/queues; rejoin and repeat must be checked. No cleanup is currently
implemented or accepted. This design intentionally does not promise all39 cells
can or should be removed, or that leaf-only work solves the visible log cluster.
